# flight-performance---POWER-BI
Power BI dashboard analyzing 540K U.S. domestic flights across 14 carriers — on-time performance, delays, cancellations, and route/airport reliability.
A Power BI project analyzing U.S. domestic flight performance for January 2025, covering 539,747 reported flights across 14 carriers and 329 origin airports. The dashboard tracks on-time performance, delay causes, cancellations, and route/airport-level reliability.

📌 Key KPIs
Total Flights: 540K
On-Time Rate: 81.2%
Delayed Arrivals: 18.8%
Cancellation Rate: 3.0%
Avg Arrival Delay: 14.2 min
Origin Airports: 329
📁 Dashboard Pages

1️⃣ Flight Overview
KPI cards for total flights, on-time rate, delayed arrivals, cancellation rate, and average delay
Daily flight volume vs. delayed arrivals trend across the month
On-time performance ranked by airline (Hawaiian & Southwest lead; Allegiant trails)
Reported delay causes broken down by hours (late aircraft & air carrier issues dominate over weather/security)
Flight outcomes donut chart (on-time, delayed, cancelled, diverted)
Busiest routes table with total flights, on-time rate, and cancellation rate (e.g., OGG→HNL, ORD→LGA, LAX→SFO)

2️⃣ Airports & Routes
Scheduled vs. completed vs. cancelled vs. diverted flight counts
Departure airport scorecard sorted by traffic (DFW, ATL, CLT, LAX, MCO, DCA, SFO, MIA) with on-time rate, cancellation rate, and average delay per airport
Arrival delay trend by scheduled departure hour (clear morning delay spike)
Flight volume and cancellations by weekday

📊 Key Insights
Hawaiian Airlines & Southwest post the best on-time performance (86% and 85%); Allegiant trails at 76%
Late aircraft and air carrier issues are the biggest drivers of delay hours — far more than weather or security
DFW and ATL handle the highest flight volumes but post below-average on-time rates (78.3% and 76.8%)
SFO and LAX stand out with strong on-time rates (86.6%, 87.1%) despite high traffic
Arrival delays spike sharply in the early morning hours, then trend upward through the day before tapering off in the evening
DCA has the highest cancellation rate among major airports (9.3%)



🧾 Conclusion

This dashboard gives a clear operational view of U.S. domestic flight performance in January 2025. While the overall on-time rate sits at a healthy 81.2%, performance varies sharply by airline and airport — with late aircraft and carrier-side issues (not weather) driving most delays. High-traffic hubs like DFW and ATL show the tradeoff between volume and reliability, while smaller/mid-size airports like SFO and LAX manage to combine high traffic with strong on-time performance.
